How it works
1. Generate your monthly content plan in your Claude project as usual.
2. Export or save it as an HTML file.
3. Upload it here — all posts are read and added to your queue as drafts.
4. Review each post, make any edits, then approve to schedule.
5. YNTP broadcast drops are flagged as manual — they appear as reminders only.
